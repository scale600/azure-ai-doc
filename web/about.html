✦ AI-First Document Processing

An AI-first pipeline for financial documents

This service prototype automates what used to be slow, manual document review. It classifies, extracts, and summarizes KYC identity documents, invoices, and service applications — and it does so with AI at every meaningful step.

The AI core

🔍
Document Intelligence Supervised DL

The "reader" — OCR and structured field extraction via prebuilt-idDocument, prebuilt-invoice, prebuilt-read.

→
✦
Azure OpenAI · gpt-4.1-mini LLM + RLHF

The "reasoner" — classification, summarization, risk and missing-field detection, with a confidence score.

Together they replace the manual work of reading, classifying, and flagging documents. This project does not train its own models — it calls these pre-trained, managed AI services for inference only.

What it does

  • Classifies documents into types (KYC ID, invoice, application).
  • Extracts key fields with confidence.
  • Summarizes content in natural language.
  • Detects risks (expirations, anomalies) and missing fields.
  • Visualizes the whole process live on a dashboard.
